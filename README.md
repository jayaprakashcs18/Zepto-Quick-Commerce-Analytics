# Zepto-Quick-Commerce-Analytics

# Project Objectives:
## Analyze Overall Performance
## Track Important KPIs
## Category Wise Sales 
## Average Discount By Category
## Inventory Value By Category 
## Out of Stock %


### Zepto Quick Commerce Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project developed using Excel, SQL, Power Query, DAX, and Power BI to analyze sales, product performance, customer orders, profitability, and operational trends.
### The project transforms raw transactional data into an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs, charts, slicers, and filters. It provides insights into sales performance, top-performing products and categories, regional trends, payment methods, quantity sold, and profitability, helping businesses make data-driven decisions related to sales, inventory, and operations.

# Key Findings
Identified top-performing products and categories based on sales and quantity sold.
Found products with high and low sales performance, helping identify demand patterns.
Analyzed regional sales trends to understand which locations contribute most to revenue.
Identified the most frequently used payment methods by customers.
Compared sales and profitability across different product categories.
Identified high-demand products that may require better inventory planning.
Found periods with higher and lower sales activity, highlighting sales trends.
The dashboard provides a clear view of overall business performance through interactive KPIs and visualizations.
Generated insights that can support inventory optimization, sales planning, and business decision-making.
